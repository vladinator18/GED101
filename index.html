Cultural Dimension

Growing up in a household rich with diverse cultures has profoundly shaped my perspective. My upbringing was a fascinating blend of Hindu beliefs and traditions, intertwined with local Filipino and Chinese cultures. Additionally, my mother's Chavacano heritage from the Zamboanga region added another distinct layer to this vibrant tapestry of influences.

This unique multicultural environment has been instrumental in cultivating a worldview characterized by openness and acceptance. It has taught me to approach new ideas and different ways of life with an inquisitive mind, fostering a deep appreciation for the myriad expressions of human culture.

Crucially, it has also instilled in me the understanding that I am not rigidly bound by any single set of traditions or beliefs. Instead, I am empowered to navigate these diverse influences, choosing which aspects resonate most deeply with my evolving self.
